Example Co Portal -- Document shelf: how to use (manual.txt)
=============================================================

This shelf holds the files staff read every day. Open one from the
portal home page, or directly with the viewer:

    /attachment?file=manual.txt

Layout of the portal, for whoever maintains it next:

    attachments/   the document shelf (what the viewer is meant to show)
    private/       portal settings. Next to the shelf, not inside it.
                   private/config.ini holds the audit token.

The viewer is only supposed to show files on the shelf. If you can
read anything from private/ through it, report it to the SRE on call.

Admin console for the SRE: /admin (JSON).
